# Recommended Content Structure for Marevita Marine

Based on analysis of Confidence Shipping, Shane Marine, and Fleet Ship.

## Core Messaging

### Tagline Options
- "Excellence in Marine Services"
- "Navigating Maritime Excellence"
- "Your Trusted Maritime Partner"
- "Precision in Every Voyage"

### Value Propositions
- Safety First: Zero-incident commitment
- Global Reach: Worldwide service network
- Expert Team: Experienced maritime professionals
- Quality Service: Industry-leading standards
- 24/7 Support: Round-the-clock availability
- Cost Effective: Competitive pricing

## Page Structure

### 1. Home Page

**Hero Section**
- Headline with value proposition
- Primary CTA (Get Quote / Contact Us)
- Secondary CTA (Learn More)
- Professional maritime imagery

**Key Metrics/Statistics**
Years of Experience, Vessels Managed, Crew Members, Successful Operations, Countries Served, Client Satisfaction Rate

**Services Overview**
4-6 main service cards with icons and brief descriptions

**Why Choose Us**
3-4 key differentiators (icon + heading + description)

**Client Testimonials**
2-3 featured testimonials, client logos, success stories

**CTA Section**
Contact form, phone/email, office locations

### 2. About Us

**Company Story**
Founding story, mission and vision, growth journey

**Our Values**
Safety, Quality, Integrity, Innovation, Customer Focus, Environmental Responsibility

**Leadership Team**
Key executives, credentials, industry expertise

**Certifications**
Industry certifications, compliance standards, awards

**Global Presence**
Office locations, service areas, network map

### 3. Services

Adapt to Marevita's actual offerings:

**Ship Management**
Technical management, crew management, safety management, vessel maintenance

**Crew Management**
Recruitment, training and development, career advancement, welfare programs

**Port Agency Services**
Port operations, documentation, logistics support, bunker arrangements

**Technical Services**
Inspections, repairs and maintenance, dry docking, equipment supply

**Consultancy**
Marine advisory, purchase guidance, project management, compliance consulting

**Emergency Response**
24/7 support, crisis management, quick response team

### 4. Fleet/Vessels

**Fleet Overview**
Types of vessels managed, fleet size, operational areas

**Vessel Categories**
Tankers, bulk carriers, container ships, specialized vessels

### 5. Safety & Quality

**Safety Policies**
Zero-incident commitment, safety management systems, training programs, emergency procedures

**Quality Standards**
ISO certifications, industry compliance, audit procedures, continuous improvement

**Environmental Responsibility**
Environmental policies, sustainability initiatives, green shipping practices

### 6. Careers

**Why Work With Us**
Company culture, career growth, training opportunities, global opportunities

**Current Openings**
Maritime positions, shore-based careers, internship programs

**Life at Marevita**
Employee testimonials, work environment, benefits

### 7. News & Insights

**Latest News**
Company updates, industry news, press releases

**Blog/Articles**
Maritime insights, industry trends, technical articles

**Case Studies**
Success stories, project highlights, client achievements

### 8. Contact

**Contact Form**
Inquiry type selection, name/email/phone, message field

**Office Locations**
Headquarters, branch offices, contact details

**Quick Contact**
Phone numbers, email addresses, emergency contacts

## Content Approach

### Tone
Professional but approachable, confident, safety-focused, global perspective, client-centric

### Guidelines
- Clear and concise (avoid jargon)
- Benefit-focused (highlight value to clients)
- Credible (use statistics and credentials)
- Visual (support with images and icons)
- Actionable (clear CTAs throughout)
- SEO-friendly

### Trust Signals
Years in business, number of clients, success rate, certifications, industry awards, client testimonials

### Visual Content
Professional photography, vessel images, team photos, office locations, service illustrations, infographics

### Interactive Elements
Contact forms, quote request tools, service calculators, live chat, newsletter signup, social media integration

## Homepage Focus
60% Services and capabilities
20% Trust signals (stats, testimonials)
10% Company information
10% Calls to action

## Implementation Priority

**Phase 1 - Essential**
Home page, About us, Services overview, Contact information

**Phase 2 - Enhanced**
Detailed service pages, Fleet information, Safety/quality pages, Leadership team

**Phase 3 - Advanced**
Case studies, Blog/news, Careers section, Client portal
