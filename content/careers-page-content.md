# Careers Page — Design & Content Strategy

## 1. Design Philosophy & Visual Language

**Core Objective:** Attract qualified, dedicated seafarers and maritime professionals by projecting trust, career longevity, safety, and prestige.

- **Theme Strategy:** Black-and-white alternating sections adhering to project standards.
- **Organic Transitions:** Organic irregular wavy SVG dividers (`SectionDivider`) between every section.
- **Interactive Role Explorer / Filtering:** Categorized role tabs or breakdown (Deck, Engine, Electro-Technical, Galley & Hotel, Cadets & Shore-based) to make navigating open positions effortless.
- **Colors & Accents:** Navy-900 backgrounds, Marine Green / Blue accents, crisp white contrast, polished badge indicators for departments.

---

## 2. Page Structure & Content Implementation

### Section 1: Hero (Black/Dark Background — #0f1318)
- **Tagline:** Build Your Career at Sea
- **Main Heading:** Careers at Sea
- **Description:** We offer opportunities for qualified and motivated maritime professionals who want to build a rewarding career in the Merchant Navy. Join a global fleet backed by industry-leading safety standards, competitive welfare, and continuous professional advancement.
- **CTA:** "Explore Opportunities" (scrolls down) & "Apply Directly" (links to contact/application form)

### Section 2: Why Sail With Marevita (White Background — #ffffff)
- **Heading:** Why Choose Marevita Marine?
- **Subtitle:** Built for Seafarers, Backed by Maritime Standards.
- **Key Pillars (4 Cards):**
  1. **Global Fleet Deployments:** Placement opportunities across modern container ships, bulk carriers, chemical tankers, cruise ships, and offshore vessels.
  2. **Safety & Crew Welfare First:** Strict adherence to MLC 2006, STCW, and IMO regulations with top-tier insurance and onboard living conditions.
  3. **Fast-Track Career Progression:** Clear rank advancement pathways from cadets to senior officers with ongoing training and certification support.
  4. **Timely Relief & Transparent Contracts:** Reliable sign-on/sign-off cycles, on-time wage remittances, and round-the-clock port support.

### Section 3: Open Positions by Department (Dark Background — #0f1318)
Categorized openings derived from authentic company requirements:

#### A. Deck Department
- **Ranks:**
  - Captain / Master
  - Chief Officer
  - 2nd & 3rd Officers
  - Deck Cadets
  - Bosun
  - Able Seaman (AB)
  - Ordinary Seaman (OS)
- **Requirements:** Valid STCW certifications, relevant sea time, valid CoC / CoP, and passport/CDC.

#### B. Engine Department
- **Ranks:**
  - Chief Engineer
  - 2nd, 3rd & 4th Engineers
  - Engine Cadets
  - Electro-Technical Officer (ETO) / Electrical Engineer
  - Fitter / Machinist
  - Motorman / Oiler / Wiper
- **Requirements:** Marine engineering qualifications, engine room watchkeeping certificates, MMD documentation.

#### C. Galley, Hotel & Hospitality
- **Ranks:**
  - Chief Cook
  - 2nd Cook / Assistant Cook
  - General Steward
  - Housekeeping & Laundry Crew
  - Cruise Hospitality & Entertainment Staff
- **Requirements:** Food handling safety certification, maritime catering certificates, cruise hospitality experience (for passenger liners).

#### D. Shore-based Marine Professionals
- **Ranks:**
  - Marine Superintendents
  - Technical Superintendents
  - Crew Manning Officers & Operations Coordinators
  - Port Captains / Fleet Inspectors

### Section 4: Application & Recruitment Process (White Background — #ffffff)
- **Step 1: Application Submission** — Send your CV and seatime documents.
- **Step 2: Document Verification & Screening** — Credentials, CDC, and STCW vetting by our technical team.
- **Step 3: Technical & Briefing Interview** — Evaluation with superintendents and vessel assignment briefing.
- **Step 4: Medicals & Flag State Endorsement** — DG-approved medical examination and flag state processing.
- **Step 5: Joining & Mobilization** — Flight booking, port agent coordination, and smooth sign-on.

### Section 5: Direct Application / Contact CTA (Dark Background — #0f1318)
- **Heading:** Ready to Join Our Fleet?
- **Body:** Send your resume and sea-time matrix to our crewing department or fill out the application form.
- **Button:** Apply Now / Contact Crewing Desk
