# Shane Marine – Services Overview  
- “Our main expertise handling is complete project of ship management.”  
- The company provides technical management, crewing, purchasing, safety & quality, and project services for various commercial vessels (general cargo, multipurpose, container, gas carriers, tugs, bunker barges, tankers, etc.).

## Crewing Management  
- Focus on identifying, training, motivating, and retaining qualified officers and crew.  
- HR division operates across three offices to manage recruitment, STCW compliance, and long‑term crew retention.  
- Emphasizes multicultural crews for cost‑effective solutions.

## Safety and Quality Management  
- Offers Document of Compliance and ISM system support as a third‑party technical manager.  
- Provides safety and quality services to vessel owners who entrust technical or crewing management to Shane Marine.

## Purchase Management  
- Controls costs by sourcing goods and services worldwide at competitive prices.  
- Aims to minimize downtime caused by stock‑outs through informed global purchasing.

## Technical Services  
- Delivers technical condition inspections for ship sale/purchase.  
- Assists prospective/new owners with ship procurement formalities.

## Other Technical Services  
- Supports clients with technical guidance related to ship assets and acquisition processes.

## Project Management  
- Guarantees safe, timely, regulation‑compliant project completion.  
- Leverages experienced teams to maximize productivity and achieve zero‑incident outcomes on complex projects.
